## Software

Our analysis pipelines are released openly. Everything below is built to run over large image
collections without manual annotation of every frame.

<div class="section-card">
<h4>AI-SCW &mdash; Automated and Integrated Seafloor Classification Workflow</h4>
<div class="pub-actions" style="margin-bottom: var(--space-3);">
<a href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-19070-2" target="_blank" rel="noopener" class="btn-pill btn-doi">Paper</a>
</div>
<p><strong>Authors:</strong> <em>B. Mbani, T. Schoening, J. Greinert</em></p>
<p>An end-to-end workflow for classifying seafloor imagery into habitat classes without hand-labelling every image.
Developed on manganese-nodule covered seabed in the Central Pacific and published in <em>Scientific Reports</em> (2022).
It is the classification backbone we are now adapting to mangrove and coastal habitats.</p>
</div>

<div class="section-card">
<h4>FaunD-Fast &mdash; Megabenthic Fauna Detector</h4>
<div class="pub-actions" style="margin-bottom: var(--space-3);">
<a href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-35518-5" target="_blank" rel="noopener" class="btn-pill btn-doi">Paper</a>
</div>
<p><strong>Authors:</strong> <em>B. Mbani, V. Buck, J. Greinert</em></p>
<p>A Faster R-CNN detector for locating and counting megabenthic fauna in optical seafloor images, trained and
evaluated on imagery from the Clarion&ndash;Clipperton Zone. Released via Zenodo and GEOMAR, and the direct ancestor
of the computer-vision component of Blue-BII.</p>
</div>

<div class="section-card">
<h4>Analysis-ready seafloor imagery, SO268 cruises</h4>
<div class="pub-actions" style="margin-bottom: var(--space-3);">
<a href="https://doi.org/10.1594/PANGAEA.957274" target="_blank" rel="noopener" class="btn-pill btn-doi">Data</a>
<a href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-023-02245-5" target="_blank" rel="noopener" class="btn-pill btn-doi">Paper</a>
</div>
<p><strong>Authors:</strong> <em>B. Mbani, J. Greinert</em></p>
<p>An open, colour-corrected and georeferenced image dataset collected along OFOS profiles during RV SONNE cruises
SO268/1 and SO268/2, published in <em>Scientific Data</em> (2023) and archived at PANGAEA.</p>
</div>
