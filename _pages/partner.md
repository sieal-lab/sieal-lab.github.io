## Partner with us

We measure carbon and biodiversity in coastal ecosystems, and turn those measurements into outputs
that hold up under scrutiny &mdash; from a verifier, a regulator, or a peer reviewer. If you have a site
that needs quantifying, or a disclosure obligation you cannot currently evidence, we would like to hear
from you.

We are a young lab and would rather say so than oversell. What we bring is not a long client list but a
set of peer-reviewed methods, published in Nature Portfolio journals and already running at scale over
hundreds of thousands of images.

<div class="section-card" markdown="1">
### Blue carbon stock assessment

Above- and below-ground carbon across mangrove extents, from Sentinel-2 optical imagery, NASA GEDI
spaceborne LiDAR and field allometry. Delivered as maps with stated uncertainty rather than a single
headline number, and structured against **Verra VM0033** so the output can support a carbon project
baseline or a re-measurement.

Currently running across 2,890 hectares at Mwache Creek, Mombasa &mdash; see
[Research]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/research).
</div>

<div class="section-card" markdown="1">
### Biodiversity integrity assessment

The Blue Biodiversity Integrity Index: a measure of ecological condition derived from below-water
stereo-video transects, analysed with computer vision rather than by hand. Designed to sit alongside
carbon figures in **TNFD** and **GRI Biodiversity Standard** reporting, where marine and coastal
exposure is typically the hardest thing to evidence &mdash; most biodiversity frameworks were built for
terrestrial ecosystems and leave you with little to report against.
</div>

<div class="section-card" markdown="1">
### Automated analysis of large image archives

Our published pipelines classify seafloor imagery and detect and count fauna without anyone annotating
every frame. If you hold a video or image archive that has never been analysed because analysing it was
not feasible, that is the specific problem we have spent years on. See
[Software]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/software).
</div>

<div class="section-card" markdown="1">
### Method development and training

Where you would rather hold the capability in-house, we build the pipeline *with* your team instead of
*for* them, and hand over the code and the documentation to run it.
</div>

### Who we work with

- **Carbon project developers** needing credible MRV for blue carbon projects
- **Conservation organisations and marine park authorities** with sites but no measurement capacity
- **Companies** facing TNFD or CSRD disclosure obligations that touch marine and coastal systems
- **Research consortia** looking for a Western Indian Ocean partner on a proposal

### How an engagement works

1. **A conversation.** Tell us the site and the question. We will say early if we think Earth observation is the wrong tool for it &mdash; sometimes it is, and finding that out cheaply is worth more than a report that hedges.
2. **Feasibility.** What the available data can and cannot support, what fieldwork would be needed, and what the uncertainty will realistically look like.
3. **Delivery.** Maps, indices and a written method, in formats your verifier or auditor can work with.
4. **Handover.** Data, code and documentation, so the work is reproducible and does not depend on us.

### On standards, plainly

We design our outputs to meet the requirements of Verra VM0033, TNFD, CSRD and the GRI Biodiversity
Standard. To be precise about what that means: we are a research group, not an accredited validation or
verification body. We produce the measurements and documentation that go *into* those processes; the
certification itself sits with the accredited bodies.

### Data and publication

Your data stays confidential. We publish methods, not client data, and we will agree what is publishable
before any work starts.
